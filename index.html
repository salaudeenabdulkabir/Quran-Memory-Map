Why this book

Your memory deserves more than guesswork.

Similar verses are one of the most persistent challenges in hifẓ. This book helps you see the difference, understand why it is there, and retain it for good.

Whether you are memorising, revising after hifẓ, teaching students, or leading prayer, My Qur’an Memory Map gives you a structured companion for stronger and more accurate recitation.

From the author

Built from years of teaching Qur’anic knowledge.

Arogundade Abdurrahmon Oladipupo is an alumnus of Risaalatur Rosuul Academy, Director of Alhusory Academy, and convener of the Eeman Revival Conference.

Driven by a profound love for the Qur’an and the legacy of Shaykh Husory, he has spent years teaching Qur’anic knowledge to students across Nigeria and internationally. He currently serves as Secretary of APWON and is pursuing a Bachelor of Science degree in Architecture.
